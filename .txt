next:

You are continuing development on “Last Lantern,” a dark fantasy idle autobattler expedition simulator.

The current destination selection system is too simple.
Right now the player selects a destination from a flat list before launching an expedition.

Replace this with:
A layered expedition world map inspired by dangerous descent and escalating risk.

GOAL:
Make destination selection itself feel strategic, atmospheric, and progression-based.

The player should feel:
“We are choosing how deep into darkness we dare to go.”

==================================================
CORE DESIGN
==================================================

Replace flat destination selection with a MAP SCREEN.

The map should contain:
- regions
- branching routes
- layered depth progression
- escalating danger tiers
- expedition risk vs reward decisions

The world should feel:
- hostile
- ancient
- partially unknown
- slowly revealed

==================================================
MAP STRUCTURE
==================================================

Design the map like descending layers into danger.

Example structure:

THE OUTSKIRTS
- abandoned roads
- low corruption
- beginner enemies

THE RUINS
- undead
- traps
- moderate corruption

THE CATACOMBS
- darkness mechanics
- sanity damage
- stronger loot

THE SUNKEN DEPTHS
- elite monsters
- environmental hazards
- rare relics

THE BLACK BELOW
- near-suicidal difficulty
- legendary rewards
- permanent corruption risk

Each deeper layer:
- increases enemy strength
- increases corruption
- improves loot quality
- introduces new mechanics

==================================================
MAP NAVIGATION
==================================================

Player should:
- unlock deeper zones gradually
- choose how far to push
- decide whether to retreat early
- weigh risk vs survival

Routes can branch:
Example:

Ruins Entrance
 ├── Forgotten Chapel
 ├── Flooded Passage
 │     └── Ancient Vault
 └── Bone Galleries

Different routes contain:
- enemy archetypes
- environmental effects
- loot biases
- event probabilities

==================================================
EXPEDITION DEPTH SYSTEM
==================================================

Depth is a core mechanic.

Deeper levels should:
- take longer
- consume more supplies
- stress heroes faster
- increase chance of death
- increase rarity of rewards

Torchlight and morale should matter more at depth.

Example:
Depth 1:
manageable

Depth 5:
heroes begin panicking

Depth 9:
corruption spreads rapidly

Depth 12:
almost nobody returns unchanged

==================================================
FOG OF WAR
==================================================

The player should NOT fully know the map.

Implement:
- hidden nodes
- unknown encounters
- unexplored branches
- scouting mechanics

Some heroes/classes improve scouting.

Examples:
- reveal adjacent nodes
- detect traps
- identify elite encounters
- locate hidden treasure

==================================================
REGION IDENTITY
==================================================

Every region should feel mechanically unique.

Examples:

THE ASH WOODS
- ambush-heavy
- low visibility
- beast enemies

THE IRON CRYPTS
- armored undead
- bleed resistance
- relic-rich

THE HOLLOW CATHEDRAL
- morale attacks
- corruption spread
- high-value relics

THE DEEP TIDE
- flooding mechanics
- movement penalties
- rare alchemy materials

==================================================
VISUAL DESIGN
==================================================

The map should feel:
- hand-drawn
- oppressive
- ancient
- functional
- readable

Avoid:
- colorful fantasy maps
- cartoon adventure visuals
- clean modern UI

Preferred style:
- parchment
- ember glow
- sketched paths
- dim node illumination
- subtle animation
- fogged unexplored areas

==================================================
NODE TYPES
==================================================

Different node categories:
- Combat
- Elite
- Treasure
- Camp
- Event
- Merchant
- Shrine
- Boss
- Corruption Zone
- Safe Exit

Nodes should visually communicate danger.

==================================================
EXPEDITION STRUCTURE
==================================================

An expedition is now:
- entering the map
- progressing node-by-node
- surviving as long as possible
- deciding when to retreat

This creates:
- tension
- emergent storytelling
- meaningful risk management

==================================================
RETREAT SYSTEM
==================================================

Retreat should become critical.

The player should frequently ask:
“Do I push deeper or leave now?”

Retreating:
- preserves heroes
- keeps partial loot
- sacrifices full-clear rewards

Greed should kill parties.

==================================================
BOSS DESIGN
==================================================

Certain deeper routes culminate in bosses.

Bosses should:
- alter regions permanently
- unlock deeper layers
- corrupt surviving heroes
- drop legendary relics

Boss encounters should feel terrifying and rare.

==================================================
LONG-TERM PROGRESSION
==================================================

As the settlement develops:
- deeper regions unlock
- scouting improves
- maps become more reliable
- expeditions travel farther
- supply management improves

The world should slowly unfold over dozens of hours.

==================================================
IMPORTANT GAME FEEL
==================================================

The map should create:
- dread
- curiosity
- temptation
- risk management

The player should stare at deeper routes and think:

“We are absolutely not prepared for that…
but the rewards could save the settlement.”

==================================================
TECHNICAL GOALS
==================================================

Create modular systems:

src/world/
- regions.ts
- mapGeneration.ts
- nodes.ts
- encounters.ts

Map nodes should support:
- procedural generation
- branching paths
- hidden information
- progression scaling

Expeditions should track:
- current node
- depth
- supplies
- torchlight
- corruption pressure
- route history

==================================================
FINAL TARGET EXPERIENCE
==================================================

The game should evolve from:
“pick a mission”

into:

“descend into a living nightmare and decide how much your heroes are worth risking.”
